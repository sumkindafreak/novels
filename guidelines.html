Community Guidelines

Community beta · Last updated 11 August 2026

Write boldly. Treat people decently. Novels is meant to give writers room for difficult fiction without turning the community itself into a hostile place.

Creative content

Fiction may explore dark, upsetting, controversial or adult themes. Writers should describe their work accurately so readers can choose what they want to read. Fictional subject matter is not the same thing as targeting real users.

Do not use the community to

Harass or threaten other users; publish private personal information; impersonate people deceptively; spam; distribute malware; coordinate unlawful activity; or upload work you do not have the right to share.

Critique and comments

Disagreement is welcome. Personal abuse is not. Discuss the writing, ideas and craft rather than attacking the writer. Repeated unwanted contact may be moderated.

Copyright

Writers retain ownership of their work. Do not copy, repost or commercially exploit another writer's work without permission.

Reporting

Use the report controls for genuine safety, rights or conduct concerns. Reports are reviewed by platform moderation. Misusing reports to harass writers may itself lead to restrictions.

Moderation

Moderation may include removing comments or content, limiting visibility, warning users or restricting accounts. Context matters, especially for fiction, satire, quotation and discussion of difficult subjects.
