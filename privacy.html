Privacy Policy

Community beta · Last updated 11 August 2026

Writelite collects only what it needs to run the community. Account and community data is stored using Supabase; the public site is delivered through GitHub Pages.

Information we process

When you create an account, the service processes your email address, authentication information, profile fields, stories, chapters, images, follows, likes, bookmarks, comments, reading progress, reports and notification state. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not stored in the Writelite application database as readable text.

Why it is used

Data is used to provide sign-in, profiles, publishing, reading, social features, moderation, security, notifications and service maintenance. Public profile and published story information is visible to visitors by design; drafts, account authentication information and private reading state are restricted by access rules.

Images and uploads

Avatars, banners and story covers uploaded through Writelite are stored in Supabase Storage. Public-facing images are intentionally accessible so they can be displayed on profiles and stories.

Security

The application uses Supabase Row Level Security to restrict database access by account ownership and role. The browser receives only a publishable API key, not an administrative service key.

Retention and deletion

Content remains until you remove it, your account is removed, or it must be retained temporarily for security, moderation or legal reasons. Account-deletion tooling is being expanded during beta; requests can be raised through the site's support/reporting process.

Third-party infrastructure

Writelite currently relies on Supabase for authentication, database and media storage, and GitHub Pages for web hosting. Those providers process technical data according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

You can edit your public profile and stories, remove user-managed content, change your password, manage follows/bookmarks, and report content. Further export/deletion controls may be added as the beta matures.
