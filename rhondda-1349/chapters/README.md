## Drafting rules retained

- Ieuan remains a close human viewpoint rather than a vehicle for historical exposition.
- The plague half remains roughly half the book's emotional foundation.
- Bed-wetting affects shame, sleeping arrangements and exposure but is never magical plague immunity.
- The bell, Dafydd's knife, Gwenllian's grave, food/work and `watch the beast before you move it` recur only when earned by earlier prose.
- The military rise remains service → mounted servant → armed retainer → man-at-arms/esquire → exceptional Poitiers service → sustainable knighthood.
- Famous history stays around Ieuan rather than making him secretly responsible for it.
- Poitiers is written locally from Ieuan's limited position, not from a false omniscient battle map.
- Edward le Despenser's 1357 patronage closes the Glamorgan circle without inventing childhood familiarity.
