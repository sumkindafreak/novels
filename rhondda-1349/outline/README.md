# Pass Four — Chapter Architecture

**Status: ADVANCED — continuity spine locked; scene build next.**

Pass Four converts the historical and character bibles into the final novel structure.

Target: **18 chapters** spanning spring 1349 to spring/summer 1357.

## Pacing rule

The book remains primarily:

1. **plague survival horror** — Chapters 1–9
2. **service / social rise** — Chapters 10–15
3. **Poitiers / consequence / knighthood** — Chapters 16–18

The military ending must not swallow the family story that made Ieuan who he is.

## Every chapter must lock

- date/season;
- Ieuan's age;
- location;
- main external problem;
- emotional change;
- skill/theme carried forward;
- continuity object/memory;
- historical evidence grading (A/B/C);
- closing beat/hook.
